San Francisco, California

Becoming an Extra in San Francisco

The Bay Area has a deep bench of established casting companies — Beau Bonneau Casting and Nancy Hayes Casting among them — and SAG-AFTRA publishes a Northern California casting directory. Film SF, the city's film commission, maintains a public database of the local industry landscape.

Last updated 2026-10-25

Background acting in San Francisco

Beau Bonneau Casting describes more than 20 years of casting in the San Francisco Bay Area, including location and extras casting for film and television, with talent registering through Casting Networks. Nancy Hayes Casting is a full-service San Francisco agency working on projects of all sizes.

SAG-AFTRA's Northern California casting page lists more than a dozen casting directors working across the Bay Area and Sacramento. Film SF — the San Francisco Film Commission — has compiled a film and media landscape database covering all nine Bay Area counties, though the city notes it does not endorse any listed vendor.

Getting started as a beginner

  • Beau Bonneau Casting asks talent to register and create a profile through Casting Networks for principal and extras work — start there rather than emailing headshots.
  • Use SAG-AFTRA's Northern California casting list to identify established casting directors, then follow each one's own registration process.
  • Keep your look current in your profiles; Bay Area commercial casting moves quickly and often books from photos.

Practical local considerations

  • The Bay Area market spans nine counties. A 'San Francisco' casting may report in Oakland, San Jose, or elsewhere — confirm the location.
  • Film SF's landscape database is informational; the city explicitly does not recommend or endorse listed vendors.
  • Northern California is a separate market from Los Angeles. LA casting notices generally do not apply to Bay Area talent.

Types of Opportunities

What Productions in San Francisco May Look For

Film and television extras

Beau Bonneau Casting lists location and extras casting for film and television among its services. Bay Area feature and series work ebbs and flows with production schedules.

Commercials, corporate, and print

Bay Area casting companies describe heavy commercial, corporate, and print volume tied to the region's advertising and technology industries.

Real-people casting

Several San Francisco companies specialize in casting real people rather than actors for commercials and campaigns — a distinct category with its own submission process.

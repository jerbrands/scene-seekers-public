Wilmington, North Carolina

Becoming an Extra in Wilmington

Wilmington’s background work runs through TW Cast & Recruit’s CastGen extras database, and the North Carolina Film Office’s official list of recently wrapped productions shows steady feature and series activity in the Wilmington region.

Last updated 2026-10-25

Background acting in Wilmington

TW Cast & Recruit uses its CastGen database for extras casting: you build a profile with current contact information, a headshot, and a full-body photo; choose the projects and roles you are available for; and confirm booking requests by text message.

The North Carolina Film Office publishes a list of productions that have wrapped within the last 18 months, and its 2025–2026 entries include several Wilmington Region features and series, among them Drivers Ed, Pretty/Ugly, A Proper Christmas, and the RJ Decker pilot and first season. Wrapped productions prove the region attracts work; they are not current openings.

Getting started as a beginner

  • Build a complete CastGen profile with up-to-date contact information, a headshot, and a full-body shot — TW Cast & Recruit says this is the most current way to see available roles.
  • Respond promptly to booking-request texts; the company confirms or releases you based on your reply.
  • Follow TW Cast & Recruit on Facebook, where it posts its most recent extras calls.

New to all of this? Start with How to Become a Movie Extra With No Experience.

Practical local considerations

  • Wilmington is a coastal city, and region-labeled productions may film outside the city itself. Check the reporting location in each booking.
  • Charlotte is a separate North Carolina production market with its own activity; do not assume a state listing concerns Wilmington.
  • The state’s recent-productions list documents completed work. Treat it as evidence of an active region, not as a casting board.

What Productions in Wilmington May Look For

Feature-film background

The state film office’s recent-wraps list includes multiple feature films shot in the Wilmington Region in 2025 and 2026. Historical wraps do not indicate current casting.

Series background

The same list records the RJ Decker pilot and first season filming in the Wilmington Region. Series work can recur, but each season casts separately.

Large-scale event production

Beast Games filmed its second season in the Eastern and Wilmington Regions, according to the state list. Large productions may have unusual role and availability requirements.

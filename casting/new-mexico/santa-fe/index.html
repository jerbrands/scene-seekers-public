Santa Fe, New Mexico

Becoming an Extra in Santa Fe

The Santa Fe Film Office maintains a dedicated page listing the background casting directors who serve northern New Mexico, and the New Mexico Film Office’s statewide casting board rounds out the picture for this distinct high-desert market.

Last updated 2026-10-28

Background acting in Santa Fe

The Santa Fe Film Office’s Casting Directors, Background, and P.A.’s page lists background casting directors serving the region — including Alessi/Hartigan, On Location Casting, Latham Casting, White Turtle Casting, and New Mexico Casting — alongside principal casting directors and talent agents.

Background contacts, principal casting directors, and talent agents serve different purposes. For non-speaking work, start with the background contacts and each company’s own performer instructions. The New Mexico Film Office’s casting board is statewide, so check whether a notice concerns Santa Fe or another location.

Getting started as a beginner

  • Use the Santa Fe Film Office’s background casting director list to find each company’s own website and registration process.
  • Check the New Mexico Film Office’s casting board for statewide notices, and confirm the work location before applying.
  • Keep current photos and accurate sizes ready, and follow the individual casting company’s instructions.

Practical local considerations

  • Santa Fe and Albuquerque are separate production markets roughly 65 miles apart. A New Mexico notice may report in either city — or somewhere else entirely.
  • The film office’s list is a directory of who works in the region. Verify each company’s current process on its own site before sharing personal information.
  • Northern New Mexico’s high-desert locations can be remote; confirm transportation and reporting details before accepting a booking.

Types of Opportunities

What Productions in Santa Fe May Look For

Feature & television background

The film office’s background-director list exists to serve productions filming in the region. A directory listing is not a list of current openings.

Roles with specific requirements

Some notices request a particular appearance, background, or skill. Describe yourself truthfully and follow the individual notice’s requirements; do not assume every background role asks for the same experience.

Statewide New Mexico notices

The state casting board covers all of New Mexico. Santa Fe and Albuquerque are separate markets about an hour apart.

Background Acting in Houston

Houston-based casting companies offer screen and commercial casting services, while the Texas Film Commission provides statewide performer notices and beginner guidance. Learn to separate a company’s talent-network intake from a project application and check whether the work really reports in the Houston area.

Last updated 2026-10-28

Background acting in Houston

Begin with Texas Film Commission casting calls and its actor guidance. Those are statewide resources; a Texas call may be far from Houston. Its actor page says applications use the email and recent photos specified in each notice, with report details supplied if hired.

GS Casting describes film, television, commercial, and branded casting from Houston. Covington Select offers a talent-network intake and describes background services across Texas from Houston headquarters. Follow a verified performer intake or project notice; a company service page is not a job application.

Getting started as a beginner

  • Check the Texas Job Hotline for current notices and apply only as the notice directs; use current photos and accurate contact details.
  • If hired, confirm pay and work dates in a written deal memo (work agreement) or contract, as the Texas Film Commission advises.
  • Treat talent-network registration as profile submission, not guaranteed consideration or employment.

Practical local considerations

  • A Houston-based company can cast projects elsewhere in Texas. Check each notice’s actual reporting location rather than using the company’s home base as the work location.
  • Confirm the reporting address, call time (required arrival time), parking or transit instructions, and whether you must arrange your own travel and lodging before accepting.
  • The Texas Film Commission’s directory of extras casting directors is a directory resource, not an active-job list.

Portland, Oregon

Becoming an Extra in Portland

Portland's background community is served by long-established local companies — Extras Only has cast in the Northwest since 1994, and Adam & the Backgrounds grew out of the Portlandia casting department. Oregon Film, the oldest state film office in the country, supports production statewide.

Last updated 2026-10-25

Background acting in Portland

Extras Only describes itself as the Northwest's longest-established full-service extras casting company, founded in Portland in 1994, with an online searchable talent database. Adam & the Backgrounds, founded by a former Portlandia casting department member, lists background casting credits on dozens of Portland-area features and series.

Oregon Film — the oldest state film office in America — administers cash rebate programs covering 25% of Oregon goods and services plus a labor rebate, and notes that Oregon's lack of sales tax stretches production budgets further. Most incentive-backed production centers on the Portland metro area, though regional uplifts encourage filming elsewhere in the state.

Getting started as a beginner

  • Register with Extras Only's online database and follow Adam & the Backgrounds for project-specific calls — the two companies represent different generations of Portland casting.
  • Read Adam & the Backgrounds' public guidance for new extras: being on time, taking direction, and treating the day as a team effort matters more than standing out.
  • Keep expectations realistic — local casting directors are explicit that background work is not a discovery shortcut, but it is a genuine education in how sets run.

Practical local considerations

  • Portland's production volume depends heavily on Oregon's incentive programs; when the state competes well for projects, background work follows.
  • Oregon's regional incentive uplifts encourage filming outside the Portland metro — a statewide notice may report hours from the city.
  • Rain is a fact of Portland production life. Exterior shoots proceed in wet weather, so confirm wardrobe and conditions before accepting a booking.

What Productions in Portland May Look For

Independent and streaming features

Adam & the Backgrounds' credit list is heavy on independent and streaming features shot in the Portland area. Credits document past work, not current openings.

Television series background

Portland has hosted multi-season series work, and local casting companies maintain standing databases for exactly this kind of recurring need.

Commercials for Northwest brands

Local casting credits include commercials for major Northwest-headquartered brands. Commercial bookings are short and frequent entry points.

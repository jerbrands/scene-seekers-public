Background Acting in Ohio

Ohio's 30% refundable Motion Picture Tax Credit has built active production communities in Cleveland, Columbus, and Cincinnati — three separate markets in one state.

Regional overview

The Ohio Motion Picture Tax Credit, administered by the Ohio Department of Development, offers a refundable 30% credit on qualifying in-state spending, with applications accepted year-round.

The Greater Cleveland Film Commission is the most visible regional office, publishing casting-agency lists, beginner guides, and a public opportunities board. Each Ohio city has its own casting community — confirm which market a notice concerns.

For current opportunities, follow the casting company's latest notices and application instructions.

San Antonio, Texas

Background Acting in San Antonio

San Antonio has a city film commission that offers project-based cast-and-crew-call promotion for eligible Greater San Antonio Metro Area productions. That is a producer submission route, not a performer registration form; performers should pair it with statewide Texas calls and verified company instructions.

Last updated 2026-10-28

Background acting in San Antonio

Film San Antonio’s official Cast & Crew Calls page states that projects with scheduled filming in the Greater San Antonio Metro Area may request complimentary promotion. Calls must be project-specific, include a script summary, and offer free public applications/auditions. This is not a standing actor signup.

The city commission also provides production-directory and destination resources; it does not promise performer bookings. Covington Select maintains a San Antonio market landing page but says its Texas markets are staffed from Houston headquarters. Machina Cinema describes a San Antonio talent base, but its casting page is production-oriented rather than a verified public extras signup.

Getting started as a beginner

  • Check city-promoted calls and state Job Hotline notices; confirm each deadline, location, compensation and submission contact.
  • The city call program is producer-facing: do not submit an actor profile to the commission as if it were an extras registry.
  • Use only free application/audition routes; the city states calls it promotes must be free and open to the public.

Practical local considerations

  • Greater San Antonio Metro Area is broader than the city boundary. Verify the actual location and your travel feasibility.
  • Check the published notice’s performer deadline and work dates. The city’s processing timetable for producers is separate from an applicant’s deadline.
  • A San Antonio landing page does not prove a local office or active role; Covington states its Texas markets are served from Houston headquarters.

What Productions in San Antonio May Look For

Greater San Antonio project calls

The city’s call-promotion process is limited to eligible projects with scheduled metro-area filming. Check the published call for its actual roles, dates, contact and terms.

Statewide Texas notices

Texas Film Commission calls can occur anywhere in the state; apply only when the location and timing work for you.

Commercial and production casting

Local-market service pages describe company capabilities, not open jobs. Do not treat a directory/profile form as an extras vacancy.

Cleveland, Ohio

Becoming an Extra in Cleveland

The Greater Cleveland Film Commission runs one of the most beginner-friendly setups of any market: a public casting-agencies page, a getting-started guide for new background actors, and an industry-opportunities board where local casting calls are posted.

Last updated 2026-10-25

Background acting in Cleveland

The Greater Cleveland Film Commission's casting agencies page lists the local companies that cast everything from starring roles to background actors, including Angela Boehm Casting, Nancy Mosser Casting, and several others. Its getting-started page points new background actors to those agencies and to its job opportunities board.

The commission also publishes practical scam warnings for Northeast Ohio actors — a sign of an active market worth approaching carefully. Ohio's 30% refundable Motion Picture Tax Credit, administered by the Ohio Department of Development, supports production across the state.

Getting started as a beginner

  • Start with the Greater Cleveland Film Commission's getting-started page, which walks new background actors through the local landscape.
  • Bookmark the commission's industry-opportunities board — local casting calls are posted there with the casting company's contact details.
  • Read the commission's scam warnings before paying anyone; legitimate casting companies do not charge talent to be considered for a specific booking.

New to all of this? Start with How to Become a Movie Extra With No Experience.

Practical local considerations

  • Northeast Ohio casting is concentrated around Cleveland, but productions film across the region — confirm whether a call is Cleveland-area or elsewhere in the state.
  • The commission warns about 'glam scams' and fake production companies targeting local actors. Verify any unexpected offer against the commission's postings.
  • Ohio's tax credit supports production statewide; it does not by itself create background work in any one city.

Types of Opportunities

What Productions in Cleveland May Look For

Feature film background

The commission's industry-opportunities board has carried paid extras calls for feature films shooting in Northeast Ohio. Each call names its casting company, dates, and pay rate.

Commercials and music videos

Recent commission postings include commercial stand-in and music video casting from local companies. These short bookings are common entry points.

Statewide incentive-backed production

Ohio's 30% refundable tax credit applies to qualifying productions statewide. Cleveland, Columbus, and Cincinnati are separate markets.

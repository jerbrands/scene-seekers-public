Background Acting in Michigan

Michigan’s background-acting resources include Real Style’s talent network and official production-support offices. Detroit is our first city guide; statewide notices can concern other regions, so check the location, role category, and submission requirements before applying.

Regional overview

Real Style describes Michigan talent profiles, extras, and commercial performers, while Detroit-based Carrie Ray Casting lists film, television, commercial, industrial, and real-people casting services. These are different routes into consideration, not a promise of available background work throughout Michigan.

The Michigan Film & Digital Media Office provides production, location, and permitting information. Its film-tour resources include Detroit, Ann Arbor, and West Michigan: those are useful geographic orientation, not evidence of current castings in every region.

Start with the Detroit guide for supported local resources. A statewide company or notice can involve travel beyond metro Detroit; confirm where the work reports and whether local-hire terms fit your situation.

Getting started safely

  • Use the official Real Style talent portal only after reading current requirements and terms. Registration is not an offer of work.
  • Separate cast notices from crew/vendor directory listings. Michigan’s professional production directory is not a general performer signup.
  • Keep recent photos and accurate sizes ready, confirm full availability, and obtain clear work and compensation terms before accepting.
  • Treat archived calls as past notices, not standing openings. Never send an application to an unrelated permitting office.

Before applying, read our casting safety guidance and beginner’s guide.

For current opportunities, follow the casting company's latest notices and application instructions.

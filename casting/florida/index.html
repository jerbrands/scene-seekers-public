Background Acting in Florida

Orlando, Miami, and Tampa are separate Florida casting markets. Use the published city guides for company resources and preparation advice, then check the role and exact reporting address before applying.

Background acting in Florida

The Orlando guide includes R.J.’s Casting and Heather Heinz Casting; Miami includes Casting TaylorMade; Tampa includes CROWDshot and a regional extras preparation guide. Follow the original company or notice instructions.

A Florida casting company may work across several cities. Check where you would report and whether the dates and travel terms fit you; theme park employment and per-project background work are separate paths.

For current opportunities, follow the casting company's latest notices and application instructions.

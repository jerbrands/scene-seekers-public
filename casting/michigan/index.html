Background Acting in Michigan

Real Style’s talent portal and Detroit-based Carrie Ray Casting offer starting points for Michigan performers. Begin with the Detroit guide, then check each notice’s role, dates, and reporting location before applying.

Background acting in Michigan

Real Style describes extras and commercial talent and offers an official Michigan talent-profile portal. Carrie Ray Casting describes film, television, commercial, and real-people casting; check whether a notice is specifically for background performers.

Use a casting company’s official performer instructions or the contact in a suitable project notice. The Michigan Film & Digital Media Office’s production resources are not a general extras registration process.

A Michigan notice may involve travel beyond metro Detroit. Confirm the address, expected availability, and whether you need to arrange your own travel and lodging before accepting.

Getting started safely

  • Use Real Style’s official talent portal after reading its current requirements and terms. Creating a profile is not an offer of work.
  • Submit only through a verified performer signup or the casting contact named in a suitable notice. Share only the information requested at that stage.
  • Keep recent photos and accurate sizes ready, confirm your availability, and obtain clear work and compensation terms before accepting.
  • Check the notice’s date and work dates. An archived call is not a standing opening.

Before applying, read our casting safety guidance and beginner’s guide.

For current opportunities, follow the casting company's latest notices and application instructions.

Becoming an Extra in Pittsburgh

Pittsburgh’s background scene runs through the Pittsburgh Film Office’s casting notices and local companies such as Movie Casting PGH, which recruits background performers for Pittsburgh production units, including the Pittsburgh unit of The Pitt.

Last updated 2026-10-25

Background acting in Pittsburgh

Movie Casting PGH maintains current-project pages — including an application page for The Pitt’s Pittsburgh unit — alongside background-talent explainers and FAQs for newcomers.

The Pittsburgh Film Office hosts a casting-notices page, a directory of casting and talent agencies, and job listings for the region. The Pennsylvania Film Office supports production statewide, so a state-level notice may concern a shoot far from Pittsburgh.

Getting started as a beginner

  • Start with the Pittsburgh Film Office’s casting-notices page and its casting and talent agency directory to see who is casting locally.
  • Read Movie Casting PGH’s background-talent FAQ before applying so you understand what productions expect from extras.
  • Apply through the specific project page or notice rather than sending general availability messages.

Practical local considerations

  • Pittsburgh and Philadelphia are separate markets on opposite sides of the state. A Pennsylvania incentive project may film hours away from either city.
  • Dated news stories about extras recruitment describe past projects. Look for a current notice or application page before assuming a production is still hiring.
  • The film office’s directories are informational listings; verify each company’s process on its own official site.

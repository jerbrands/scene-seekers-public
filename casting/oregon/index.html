Background Acting in Oregon

Portland’s Extras Only and Adam & the Backgrounds offer starting points for aspiring background actors in Oregon. Use their official performer instructions and confirm the actual location of each project.

Background acting in Oregon

Extras Only offers a talent database, while Adam & the Backgrounds publishes project-specific guidance and calls. Registration and submissions are not bookings.

An Oregon notice may involve travel beyond Portland, including coastal or inland locations. Confirm the reporting address, dates, and travel arrangements before committing.

For current opportunities, follow the casting company's latest notices and application instructions.

Background Acting in Austin

Austin’s film commission points readers to local employment tools and Texas’s statewide Job Hotline. For a first background role, separate performer notices from crew jobs, check the filming location, and understand the submission terms before committing.

Last updated 2026-10-25

Background acting in Austin

The Austin Film Commission’s Employment Tools page links to local casting-director directories and the Texas Film Commission Job Hotline. Its production directory is for local crew and vendors; it is not a general background-performer signup.

The Texas Film Commission offers a separate guide to starting as an actor and a Casting Calls board for actors, talent, and extras. The board covers all Texas, so an Austin reader should check each project’s work location rather than assuming a statewide listing is local.

Getting started as a beginner

  • Use the Casting Calls category rather than a crew or technical-job listing when looking for performer work.
  • The state actor guide recommends sending recent photos and contact information to the address in the notice. Follow any additional project-specific instructions.
  • Confirm dates and compensation in a written deal memo or contract before committing. The state guide recommends written terms even for unpaid or volunteer work.

Practical local considerations

  • Use Austin’s Employment Tools page to orient yourself locally, then check the precise location and dates on a statewide Hotline notice.
  • Texas Hotline rules exclude student projects receiving course credit. The board is not a complete picture of every educational or independent project around Austin.
  • Hotline rules prohibit application and audition fees. They are the board’s rules, not a guarantee that every acting advertisement elsewhere follows them.

New Orleans, Louisiana

Becoming an Extra in New Orleans

New Orleans has two established full-service casting companies with deep local roots — Caballero Casting and Coulon Casting — plus a city film office. Both companies cast background performers alongside principal roles.

Last updated 2026-10-28

Background acting in New Orleans

Caballero Casting, owned by Brent Caballero, CSA, offers principal and extras casting for feature films, television shows, commercials, and music videos, with offices in New Orleans and Baton Rouge.

Coulon Casting casts principal actors and background extras for feature, television, and commercial productions across the Southern region. For a beginner, the useful next step is each company’s official performer instructions—not a general production directory.

Getting started as a beginner

  • Visit each casting company’s official website to find its current registration process rather than relying on third-party reposts.
  • Because local companies also serve Baton Rouge and the wider South, check the work location in every notice before assuming it is a New Orleans shoot.
  • Prepare recent photos and accurate sizes, and follow the specific submission instructions in each notice.

Practical local considerations

  • New Orleans and Baton Rouge are separate production markets roughly 80 miles apart. A company with offices in both may cast for either.
  • Regional casting scope means a New Orleans-based company’s project could film in another state. Confirm the location before committing.
  • Keep recent photos and clothing sizes ready so you can follow a suitable notice’s requested format without rushing or guessing.

What Productions in New Orleans May Look For

Feature & television background

Both Caballero and Coulon describe feature and television extras casting among their services. Service descriptions are not lists of current openings.

Commercials & music videos

Caballero Casting includes commercials and music videos in its extras work. Check each company’s current notices for actual projects.

Regional Southern productions

Coulon Casting describes work across Louisiana, Texas, Mississippi, Alabama, Florida, and Georgia. A New Orleans company’s notice may concern a shoot elsewhere in the region.

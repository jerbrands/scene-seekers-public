New York City, New York

Becoming an Extra in New York City

New York City supports background work through established casting companies and a dedicated city film office. AND Casting runs a free talent database from its New York and New Jersey offices, while the Mayor’s Office of Media and Entertainment supports production across the five boroughs.

Last updated 2026-10-25

Background acting in New York City

AND Casting describes itself as specializing in sourcing local background extras for feature films and television series, with offices in New York and New Jersey. Its talent database is free to join, and the company says it works with SAG, union, and non-union talent.

The Mayor’s Office of Media and Entertainment includes the city’s Film Office and reported continued support for film and television production in its 2025 year-end summary. A city film office is a production-facing agency, not a casting service, so performer applications go through casting companies rather than the city.

Getting started as a beginner

  • Create a free profile in AND Casting’s talent database and keep your photos, sizes, and contact details current.
  • Read each notice’s reporting location carefully. A New York-area project may report anywhere in the five boroughs or across the river in New Jersey.
  • Follow the submission instructions in the individual notice rather than sending a general application to a city office.

Practical local considerations

  • The New York market spans five boroughs, and some New York-based casting companies also cast New Jersey projects. Confirm where you would actually report before accepting a booking.
  • Early call times are common in background work generally; check whether you can reach the reporting location at the requested time before committing.
  • The Mayor’s Office of Media and Entertainment supports and promotes production; it does not register or book background performers.

What Productions in New York City May Look For

Feature-film & series background

AND Casting identifies feature films and television series as its background-casting focus. That describes the company’s services, not a list of current openings.

Union & non-union roles

The company states it works with SAG, union, and non-union talent. Individual productions set their own eligibility requirements.

Commercial work

AND Casting’s featured work includes commercial projects alongside features. Check each notice for its own dates, location, and requirements.

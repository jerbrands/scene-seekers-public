Chicago, Illinois

Becoming an Extra in Chicago

Chicago’s own city film office publishes a casting-information page naming the background contacts for its long-running television series and a list of established local casting agencies. It is the most reliable starting point for a Chicago beginner.

Last updated 2026-10-25

Background acting in Chicago

The City of Chicago Film Office’s Current Production and Casting Information page lists separate background contacts for Chicago Fire, Chicago Med, and Chicago PD, including dedicated email addresses, Facebook pages, and a mailing list that sends casting notices directly.

The same page lists Chicago casting agencies — including 4 Star Casting, Cine-Casting, Extra Ordinary Casting of Chicago, Joan Philo Casting, Marinella Hume Casting, and O’Connor Casting — and points readers to the Illinois Film Office Production Guide and SAG-AFTRA Chicago. A city-published list is a starting point for your own verification, not a booking channel.

Getting started as a beginner

  • Start at the city film office’s casting page and use the contact listed for the specific show you are interested in — each series handles background separately.
  • Sign up for the mailing list the city page links to so new notices reach you directly, and follow the named Facebook pages for the latest posts.
  • Keep recent photos and accurate sizes ready, and never pay a fee simply to apply for background work.

Practical local considerations

  • Series background contacts are show-specific. An email address for one production is not a general Chicago extras registration.
  • Chicago productions film across the city’s neighborhoods, so confirm the reporting location and your transportation before accepting a booking.
  • The city’s agency list is informational. Verify any company’s current process on its own official site before sharing personal information.

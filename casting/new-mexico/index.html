Background Acting in New Mexico

Wide desert skies, mountain backdrops, and adobe architecture give New Mexico a look that productions travel for — and Albuquerque has become a familiar name in modern television and film.

Regional overview

Albuquerque and Santa Fe are the state's best-known production areas. Westerns, crime dramas, and science fiction have all made use of New Mexico's landscapes.

For travelers, New Mexico pairs well with a Southwest road trip — just remember that casting is never guaranteed.

For current opportunities, follow the casting company's latest notices and application instructions.

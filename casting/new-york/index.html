Background Acting in New York

New York's streets, subways, and skylines have appeared in countless films and shows. Productions here often need people who look like everyday New Yorkers going about their day.

Regional overview

New York City is the center of production activity in the state, with television series, features, and commercials filming throughout the five boroughs.

Before applying, check the exact reporting location and whether you can reach it at the requested time. A notice for the New York area may involve travel beyond your usual commute.

For current opportunities, follow the casting company's latest notices and application instructions.

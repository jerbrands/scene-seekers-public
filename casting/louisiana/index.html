Background Acting in Louisiana

From the balconies of New Orleans to the bayous beyond, Louisiana offers settings that are hard to replicate anywhere else.

Background acting in Louisiana

New Orleans and Baton Rouge have both hosted film and television productions. Period pieces, music-driven stories, and Southern settings are frequent visitors.

Check the reporting location rather than assuming a shoot is near the city named in a notice. Confirm travel arrangements and any reimbursement terms before accepting work.

For current opportunities, follow the casting company's latest notices and application instructions.
